voidshell
Final monochrome logo package

START HERE
vector/ contains the symbol, horizontal lockup and stacked lockup in SVG,
PDF and EPS. Each is available in black and white. The logo artwork in these
files consists entirely of vector paths. The empty center, curved cuts and
terminal detail are real cutouts, not background-colored shapes.

raster/ contains matching 3200-pixel-wide PNGs with transparent backgrounds,
black artwork on white, white artwork on black, and JPG previews.

icons/black/ and icons/white/ contain transparent PNGs at 16, 32, 48, 180,
192 and 512 pixels, plus a favicon.ico containing 16, 32 and 48 pixel images.
The filename color refers to the artwork, not the background.

icons/app/ contains opaque black tiles with white artwork at 180, 192 and
512 pixels, plus a relative-path web app manifest. The 180-pixel tile can be
used as an Apple touch icon. Move the manifest and its icons together.

SMALL SIZES
The 16-pixel symbol uses one wider channel. The 32 and 48 pixel symbols use
two wider channels. These optical versions omit the terminal cutout.
Use the full symbol at 64 pixels and above. The micro and tiny SVGs are
included if you need to reproduce those small-size treatments.

EDITABLE SOURCE
source/voidshell-editable-master.svg contains the symbol and individually
outlined wordmark letters. Edit the paths in Illustrator, Inkscape, Figma
or another SVG editor. No font installation is needed to display or edit
the vector outlines. Text is outlined, not live typeset text.
A native Adobe Illustrator .ai file is not included. SVG is the editable
source format; the PDF and EPS files also contain editable vector artwork.

DESIGN
The full symbol uses a 400-unit circular exterior and a 224-unit circular
void. Two 10-unit curved cuts form the right-side shell layers. The terminal
cutout uses the same 10-unit weight and 45-degree chevron geometry.
The wordmark uses outlined URW Gothic Demi forms with adjusted spacing and
a circular i dot. Colors are pure black (#000000) and white (#FFFFFF).

USAGE
Use black artwork on a light background and white artwork on a dark one.
Keep a clear margin of at least 1/8 of the symbol diameter around the logo.
Preserve its proportions. Use the supplied optical icons at favicon sizes.
For web SVGs, preserve the viewBox when changing width or height.
White vector files can appear blank in viewers with white backgrounds.

PRESENTATION
voidshell-presentation.pdf, .svg and .png show the same final artwork in
both colors: horizontal lockup, standalone symbol, stacked lockup, and the
16, 32 and 48 pixel favicon versions. The small raster icons embedded in the
presentation are previews; the six logo vector exports have no raster images.
